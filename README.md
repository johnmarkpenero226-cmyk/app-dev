# Naruto

## About the Series
**Naruto** is one of my favorite anime series created by *Masashi Kishimoto*. It follows the journey of Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja who dreams of becoming the Hokage, the strongest leader of his village.

### Why I Like Naruto
- Inspiring story
- Amazing character development
- Exciting battles
- Valuable life lessons about friendship and perseverance

### Main Characters
1. Naruto Uzumaki
2. Sasuke Uchiha
3. Sakura Haruno
4. Kakashi Hatake
